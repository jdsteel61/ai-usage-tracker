Spike alerts

A red ! marks a card when a quota jumps sharply within one polling interval, often a runaway agent loop. It fires on a rise of at least Jump points, or of at least Relative min points at a rate above the 24-hour median rate times Rate. Resets, first readings and long gaps never alert.

API keys

Keys are stored only in Windows Credential Manager, never in settings files or logs. Store, Test and Remove each ask to confirm first.
